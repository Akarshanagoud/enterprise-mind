# Sales Compensation Plan FY26

Account executives earn a base commission of 8 percent on new annual contract value. Deals above 250,000 dollars earn an accelerated rate of 11 percent. Commission is paid quarterly, 45 days after the quarter closes.

Clawbacks apply if a customer cancels within the first 90 days. Plan changes require approval from the Chief Revenue Officer.

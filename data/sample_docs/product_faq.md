# Product FAQ

Atlas is our entry-level analytics plan for small teams. Beacon adds real-time dashboards and alerting. Cipher includes advanced security controls such as customer-managed encryption keys. Delta is the enterprise plan with a dedicated success manager.

Delta customers receive a first response from support within 1 hour, 24 hours a day. Cipher customers receive a first response within 4 business hours. Atlas and Beacon customers receive a first response within 1 business day.

All plans can be cancelled at any time from the billing page. Annual plans are billed upfront and renew automatically unless cancelled 14 days before renewal.

# Incident Response Runbook

A SEV1 incident is a complete outage or a confirmed data breach. SEV1 incidents require an incident commander to be assigned within 15 minutes and a status page update every 30 minutes. A SEV2 incident is a partial outage affecting a subset of customers.

After every SEV1 or SEV2 incident, the owning team must publish a blameless postmortem within 5 business days. Postmortems must list root causes, customer impact and follow-up actions with owners.

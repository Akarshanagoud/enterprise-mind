# Travel and Expense Policy

Employees may spend up to 75 dollars per day on meals while travelling. Receipts are required for every expense above 25 dollars. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.

Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Hotel bookings above 250 dollars per night require approval from the department head. Personal entertainment and upgrades are not reimbursable.

# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of approval. Digital products that have been downloaded are not eligible for a refund unless they are defective.

To request a refund, contact support with the order number. Support agents must verify the order before approving any refund above 500 dollars. Refunds above 2,000 dollars require approval from a finance manager.
